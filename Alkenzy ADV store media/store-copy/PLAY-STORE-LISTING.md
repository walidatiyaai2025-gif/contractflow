## English (en-US)
**Short description (73/80):**  
Manage contracts, customers, payments and follow-ups in one business app.

**Full description:**  
Alkenzy ADV is a business contract-operations app that brings day-to-day contract work into one mobile interface.

Authorized users can access:
- Business and financial dashboards
- Customers and suppliers
- Contracts and contract details
- Payments and collections
- Follow-ups and notifications
- Reports and exports
- Profile and account settings

The app requires an authorized account and connects to the organization’s Alkenzy ADV service to access permitted business data over a secure connection.

Alkenzy ADV is designed for operational contract management. It is not a banking, investment, lending, or consumer financial-services app.

Available features may vary according to user permissions and organization settings.
